Expert Advice

Plant Care Tips

Essential guidelines from our experts to keep your greenery thriving.

Smart Watering

Check soil moisture before watering. Most plants prefer the top inch of soil to dry out between waterings to prevent root rot.

Right Sunlight

Place indoor plants near bright, indirect light. Outdoor plants should be positioned based on their specific full-sun or shade requirements.

Regular Pruning

Remove dead or yellowing leaves periodically. This redirects the plant's energy to new growth and maintains a healthy shape.

Proper Nutrition

Feed plants with a balanced organic fertilizer during their active growing season for lush foliage and vibrant blooms.
